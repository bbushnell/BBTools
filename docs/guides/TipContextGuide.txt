Experimental contig-tip polishing
================================

This experimental workflow is OFF in ordinary Tadpole, TadPipe and consensus
runs. tippolish.sh is an explicit standalone driver; it scans every contig and
runs the guarded workflow without truth labels or a reference genome. Assembly
defaults, including mincountextend, remain unchanged. It is a targeted tip repair
method, not a general whole-assembly consensus replacement.

Standalone driver
-----------------
tippolish.sh assembly=contigs.fa in=paired.fq.gz interleaved=t out=polished.fa
tippolish.sh assembly=contigs.fa in=R1.fq.gz in2=R2.fq.gz out=polished.fa
tippolish.sh assembly=contigs.fa in=single.fq.gz out=polished.fa -Xmx8g t=8

Use adapter-trimmed, filtered, quality-calibrated, preferably deduplicated reads
BEFORE correction/merging/extension. The driver does not deduplicate. Inferred
extension bases and multiple PCR copies are not independent evidence. Omitting
in2 and interleaved=t selects single-end input, unlike TadPipe's paired default.

The assembly must have unique whitespace-free names. Inputs are left unchanged;
the supplied assembly is the retained unpolished version. Output is a new FASTA
(.gz supported) and report= defaults to <out>.edits.tsv. Existing outputs are
refused unless overwrite=t; input/output aliases remain forbidden. Inputs must
be files, not streams or comma lists. Standard input/output aliases and t=auto
use the native Parser; unsupported child-tool options are rejected explicitly.
temp= selects an existing parent for a unique work directory. Commands, logs,
timings, models and all evidence are retained on success and failure. The driver
does not automatically remove potentially useful failed-run evidence.

The driver defaults to minoverlap=33 maxoverlap=200 indexed=t. It retains the
16bp anchors and mandatory original32mer veto. Targets with ambiguous bases
are retained unchanged because their complete original-kmer evidence cannot be
queried. No-context assemblies pass through without read mapping/counting.

Each stage runs in a separate JVM, sequentially, with t= threads and -Xmx per
child (8g by default). The coordinator uses256MB. childheap= can also be placed
in config= files. Whole-assembly mapping is shared once, but context references
are deliberately mapped separately to preserve the tested mapping competition.
This first driver still counts read kmers per eligible target and rescans the
whole mapping for each target. Large numbers of contexts can be expensive;
it is not yet a scalable metagenome polisher. The lower-level commands below
remain available for inspecting or reproducing individual cases.

Initial validation on Spirulina used simulated 10x Q30 paired 150bp reads at
K32 with mce=1: three of fourteen residual substitutions were repaired.
A subsequent frozen panel covered 12 other bacterial genomes at 10x/20x/40x,
using the same read-error and assembly settings. All contigs were inspected,
not only known errors. Six ends in two 10x libraries had qualifying contexts;
three substitutions were repaired, reducing 101 errors to 98, with zero newly
wrong bases. The 24 libraries at 20x/40x already had zero reported substitutions.
Contig count, assembly length, aligned bases, genome fraction, NGA50 and
global/local misassemblies were unchanged. The benefit is small, not a general
polishing solution. Designed allele/repeat controls are not additional genomes.

An exploratory rerun of the same36 libraries lowered only minoverlap to33,
retaining16bp mismatch anchors and all read/kmer safeguards. It repaired27
substitutions (101->74), including the original three, with zero newly wrong
bases. Every other paired QUAST field was unchanged. Two designed minority
controls at newly repaired sites retained both original alleles. This reused
panel motivated the setting; it is not independent confirmation of it.

A separate confirmation froze12 different bacterial genera and read seeds
before generating their10x libraries. Without changing the33bp rule, it repaired
18 substitutions (97->79), with zero newly wrong bases. Every other paired
QUAST field was unchanged. These genomes were disjoint from the first12 but
had appeared in an earlier assembly benchmark; independence here concerns the
polishing rule, not the entire assembler. Across the two panels there were45
repairs and no observed new errors. Both panels use synthetic isolate reads;
this does not establish safety for natural mixed-strain libraries.

Important limitations
---------------------
Use originalkmers= for the mapping-independent safeguard. Without it, four
designed genuine minority/repeat cases were wrongly homogenized even though
their libraries contained two or four supporting reads: whole-assembly mapping
had diverted the support elsewhere. The kmer safeguard prevented these four
changes while retaining the three biological repairs.

A true one-read allele can STILL be overwritten: its observed evidence can be
identical to an error. End-overlap uniqueness within the tested bounds is not
genomic uniqueness. Kmer counts are occurrences, not independent deduplicated
molecules. Shared words can conservatively prevent legitimate repairs. No
metagenome safety claim is made. Two additional designed tests added distinct
minority-allele read pairs at the newly repaired Gilvibacter sites in its full
read background; both original alleles were preserved. These targeted controls
do not establish a false-correction rate in natural mixed-strain libraries.
Broader real-library and mixed-genome validation remains needed.

The associated ordinary consensus fixes normalize reverse-strand bases and
qualities before accumulation and enforce mindepth for deletions and the
selected insertion allele. These correctness fixes are not optional polishing.
Deletion quality now uses the actual previous/next query bases, avoiding an
overrun before the final base. Accepted leading deletions use the first quality.
Match strings ending in a deletion remain unsupported by the identity parser;
this repair does not change that existing input restriction.

Workflow (run from the BBTools installation directory)
-----------------------------------------------------
Keep the original assembly and reads immutable. Use unique whitespace-free
FASTA names. selected.bed contains exact target names in its first tab-separated
column; coordinates are ignored. Both ends are scanned without truth bases.

bash dev/tipcontextpanel.sh mode=prepare in=assembly.fa targets=selected.bed dir=panel

The directory must be new. targets.tsv gives case names, lengths, roles and
left/right offsets; -1 means no qualifying context. Unique first-level donors
are included as controls. Each case contains original.fa, target.bed and any
left.context.fa/right.context.fa. This scan retains whole contigs in memory.

Preparation accepts minoverlap=60 maxoverlap=200 and indexed=f by default.
To experiment with the33bp minimum, pass minoverlap=33 to BOTH prepare and
tipcontextguard.sh. Mismatch anchors remain fixed at16bp each. The optional
indexed=t preparation mode uses exact oriented16mer donor prefixes to avoid
impossible comparisons; complete-overlap verification and physical duplicate
placement counting are unchanged. It produced byte-identical metadata and
original/context sequences on all36 libraries. It adds primitive prefix lists
and a lazy reverse-complement cache, not another read kmer table.

On a2944-contig four-copy scaling fixture, exhaustive/indexed preparation took
152.30/32.53 wall seconds and127.37/2.48 user CPU seconds, respectively. This is
one same-host t=1 pair including output I/O, not a universal speedup claim.
Both methods passed at16/32/64/128MB heaps on a separate655-contig assembly;
16MB is the lowest tested success, not a universal or exact minimum. Repeated
read mapping and original-kmer counting remain separate pipeline costs.

For each case with a context:
1. Map reads to the WHOLE original assembly. Extract that target's primary
   alignments, for example with samstreamer.sh bed=target.bed. Run consensus.sh
   on those alignments against original.fa with outm=original.alm.
   Do not use isolated-target mapping as the original support evidence.
2. Map the same reads separately to the context FASTA and make its own .alm.
   Use unpadded references and mafsub=0.5 mindepth=2 noindels=t trimdepth=0
   trimns=f for both consensus calls. Use -Xss64m if graph serialization needs
   a larger stack. Only load trusted locally generated .alm files; these are
   serialized Java objects, not a safe untrusted interchange format.
3. Query original-allele 32mers directly from the original reads. For a target
   named contigA of length632, a complete query is:

   bash dev/kmerwindowdepth.sh in=reads.fq.gz ref=panel/case001/original.fa scaffold=contigA regions=32-632 k=32 out=original.kmers.tsv t=4 -Xmx8g

   Use in2= for split pairs or interleaved=t for interleaved pairs. regions=
   specifies one-based inclusive KMER ENDS. The query FASTA contributes no
   counts. Querying the full target ensures complete evidence; selected end
   ranges are also possible. Unknown bases in queried windows are rejected.
4. Apply the guard explicitly. For a right context with offset0:

   bash dev/tipcontextguard.sh originalmodel=original.alm contextmodel=right.alm assembly=assembly.fa originalkmers=original.kmers.tsv offset=0 side=right out=right.guarded.fa report=right.decisions.tsv

   Replace paths with the case paths. For a left context use side=left and
   its left_offset from targets.tsv. Each model must contain one graph. The
   original target must be unchanged at offset within the context reference.
   Output files must be new. Per-context remapping can be expensive.

Guard defaults: side=right, minoverlap=60, maxoverlap=200; offset is required.
The fixed policy requires one compatible end placement, at most one mismatch
with16 exact bases on each side, donor agreement, context support>=2 and
original mapped support<2. The reference pseudocount is excluded.

With originalkmers=, any overlapping original32mer at depth>=2 preserves the
base. Otherwise every overlapping query must be present before a change is
allowed. Missing evidence abstains; measured zero is not missing. Omitting
originalkmers= is only for reproducing the weaker alignment-only comparison.

Write each prepared side to panel/caseNNN/left.guarded.fa or right.guarded.fa,
including donor controls. Then reconstruct the complete unchanged-length assembly:

bash dev/tipcontextpanel.sh mode=merge in=assembly.fa dir=panel out=guarded.fa report=edits.tsv

All records, names and order are retained. Opposing substitutions abstain.
Compare full original/guarded assemblies against the same truth, if available.
The optional audit accepts native QUAST substitution-coordinate files:

bash dev/tipcontextpanel.sh mode=grade in=edits.tsv before=original.used_snps after=guarded.used_snps out=summary.tsv

Indels and unexplained mismatch-locus changes are rejected. Report repairs and
new damage separately alongside missing sequence and structural metrics.
This audit is not a general QUAST identifier-conversion utility.
